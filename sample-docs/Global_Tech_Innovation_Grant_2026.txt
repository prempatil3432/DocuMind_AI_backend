1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & PROGRAM OBJECTIVES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Global Tech Innovation & Climate Resilience Grant (GTICR-2026) provides up to
$250,000 USD in non-dilutive capital grants to qualified technology organizations.
The program accelerates auditable, mission-critical technologies focused on sustainable
computing, artificial intelligence integrity, and distributed resilience systems.
Applicants must follow all mandatory guidelines, provide verifiable proof of claims,
and adhere strictly to the schedule outlined in Section 3.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA & APPLICANT QUALIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To qualify for funding consideration, applicant entities must satisfy each condition:
- Organization must be legally incorporated and in continuous good standing.
- Maximum headcount cannot exceed 500 full-time equivalents as of the submission date.
- Applicant must hold full, uncontested ownership of primary software patents and IP.
- Must demonstrate active, verified commercial or pilot deployment of target software.
- Entities currently undergoing bankruptcy, active litigation, or sanctions are ineligible.

4. MANDATORY DOCUMENTATION & SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All applicant dossiers must include the following verified attachments:
- Audited Financial Statements: Certified balance sheets for fiscal years 2024 and 2025.
- Technical Architecture Blueprint: System architecture, threat model, and data flow (max 20 pages).
- Intellectual Property Affidavit: Legal certification of software ownership signed by legal counsel.
- Risk Mitigation & Ethics Declaration: Compliance with data privacy and ethical AI standards.
- Executive Leadership Bios: One-page resumes for each key principal investigator.
